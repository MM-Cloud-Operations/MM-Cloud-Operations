How We Work

The principles that shape how we hire, and how we expect to work together.

Ownership Over Tickets

We hire people who can own a client relationship and a system end-to-end, not just close out assigned tasks. You'll have direct client contact from day one.

Remote & Flexible

We care about the quality of the work and how you communicate with clients, not where or exactly when you do it.

Merit-Based, Open to Everyone

We evaluate people on skill, judgment, and how they work with clients — not background. We welcome applicants of any race, gender, age, religion, disability status, or nationality.

Depth Over Headcount

We're growing slowly and intentionally. We'd rather bring on the right person a bit later than fill a seat quickly.

No Open Roles Right Now

We don't have specific positions posted at the moment — we're a small team and we hire when the work genuinely calls for it. If you think you'd be a strong fit for how we work, we'd still like to hear from you.
