Client Story

Connecting a Banking Platform to Salesforce, Without the Risk

One client needed their customer-facing banking platform to feed directly into Salesforce — without ever exposing their core database to outside systems. Here's what we built:

  • Every new signup flows into Salesforce automatically, in real time.
  • Duplicate records are caught and merged automatically, so reps never work the same lead twice.
  • If anything in the pipeline breaks, our team is notified immediately — before it becomes the client's problem.

The result: a fully automated intake process the client's team hasn't had to think about since.
